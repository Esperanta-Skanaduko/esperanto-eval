esperanto-eval

A benchmark of Esperanto grammar and word-building for language models. 577 questions in eight tasks, each with one correct answer that follows from a rule of the language, generated from a seed so anyone can audit or regenerate it.

  • Auditable keys
  • Any model
  • 95% intervals
  • MIT
v0.1: not yet reviewed by native speakers. The answer keys come from standard rules and are checked by code, but no fluent speaker has gone through them. Help review them.
